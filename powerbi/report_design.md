# Report Design

## Page 1 - Executive Overview
Cards: Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Total Quantity.
Visuals: monthly sales trend, category sales, region/store sales.

## Page 2 - Product Performance
Category/product sales, quantity, discount impact and product ranking.

## Page 3 - Store Performance
Sales by region/store, order count and average order value.

## Page 4 - Customer Analysis
Sales/orders by customer, geography and customer contribution.

Use dim_date for all time slicing.
